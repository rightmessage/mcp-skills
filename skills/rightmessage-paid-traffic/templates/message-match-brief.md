# Message-match brief

## Scope

- Exact project, destination URL, approved offer and confirmed conversion:
- Build scope and unchanged invariants (ads, budgets, offer terms, form, attribution):
- Draft/production baseline; unrelated edits; owner/approver:

## Signal contract

Record explicit key and allowed values, attribution kept intact, redirects/encoding, precedence, lifetime, repeated/conflicting keys, missing/invalid behavior, lifecycle exclusions, and any approved legacy fallback. Mark proposed tokens as proposed, not deployed.
